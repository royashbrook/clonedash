# Published content

Add an accepted submission here as one file. See [Publish a submission](../README.md#publish-a-submission) for paths, formats and release checks. Empty shelves stay hidden.

### Rights for new public submissions

Saving, backing up, or exporting a creation is not submitting it. For a **new public submission**, the creator keeps ownership. We accept original levels, skins, loops and songs under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) with affirmative permission for the specific revision. This permits sharing, remixing and commercial use by others, with credit and an indication of changes. The license cannot be withdrawn from people who follow its terms.

Before accepting a revision, show exactly what will be published, including embedded music. Ask the rights holder or authorized submitter to affirm:

> I have the rights to submit this creation. I license my original contributions in this revision under CC BY 4.0. My public credit is ____. The sources, required credits and changes for anything I reused are ____ (or “none”).

The maintainer keeps the permission record with the submission ID, revision and terms version; it is not a public list of accounts. Publish the title, chosen credit, license link, source and changes with the creation in credits available to players, including offline. If an embedded loop, composition, arrangement, or sample has unclear rights or incompatible terms, hold the submission. Do not ask for a real name for the credit. Do not ship a contribution if the required credits cannot travel with it.

This is not a blanket relicense: the [software license](../LICENSE), existing content grants, and [third-party music notices](../public/music/credits.html) remain as they were. The public submission license does not apply to private backups, drafts, comments or scores.
