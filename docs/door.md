# door

Pick **PORTALS → DOOR**. Once through the amber portal, tap to hop like the square. Keep the same press down from takeoff to rise higher, up to about 4.5 blocks. If you let go, another press in midair does not bring the lift back. Holding through landing starts the next jump. Upside down, the ceiling is your ground.

Door has the square's collisions. It does not turn entering a portal in midair, a ring bounce, or a booster into an extended jump. Those keep their own speeds and gravity. The portal is a new object id; an older game refuses a trail containing it. Nothing changes in an existing trail or share code.

**Try The Tall Door:** import the code below, play, and press as you pass through the first portal. A tap hits the four-block wall; holding until you clear it finishes the trail. To build it yourself: use length 24, height 9, a Door portal at (3,0), and a column of four blocks at x=8.
